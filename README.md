### Hi, I'm Dipseka

> *Researcher, founder, and someone who thinks the best ideas live at unexpected intersections.*

I'm a Noise Analyst by day, founder by night. I built [Sarva](https://github.com/dipsekatimsina-sarva) -- an AI platform for immigrant-owned ethnic grocery stores -- because the communities I come from deserve tools that actually work for them.

My research background is all over the place in the best way: I've modeled wildfires in Montana, done night SCUBA dives in the Caribbean to film octopuses, analyzed forest biomass across the southern U.S., and worked with NASA during a global pandemic. I like hard problems and unexpected places.
